Get your WhatsApp contacts, groups and chats out.

WAVault is a free browser extension for WhatsApp Web and WhatsApp Business. Export every contact to Excel, CSV, vCard, Google Contacts or Outlook, pull the member list of any group you are in, back up whole chats with their photos and voice notes, and save statuses. It runs inside your own browser and sends nothing anywhere.

WhatsApp holds your contacts. It has no export button.

WAVault adds the buttons that are missing, and merges everything WhatsApp scattered into one clean list.

  • Customers trapped in a chat appSmall businesses run on WhatsApp Business but cannot get their customer list, sorted by label, into a spreadsheet or CRM.
  • Contacts split across years of phonesSwitching phones and syncing devices leaves the same person in the address book, in chats and in groups, often twice.
  • Group lists you cannot downloadAdmins of schools, clubs and communities need member lists for attendance, invitations or a newsletter.
  • Chats you want to keepThe phone exports one chat at a time. Statuses vanish after a day.

Install in two minutes

  1. Download

    Get the zip for your browser from the latest release and unzip it into a folder you keep.

  2. Load it

    Open your browser's extensions page, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the folder.

  3. Open WhatsApp Web

    Log in at web.whatsapp.com and click the WAVault tab on the right edge.

Questions

How do I export all my WhatsApp contacts to Excel?

Install WAVault, open WhatsApp Web, click the WAVault tab on the right edge, stay on All contacts, choose Excel in the format menu and click Export.

Can I export the members of a WhatsApp group?

Yes, for every group you are a member of. Open Groups, click Load member lists, tick the groups and export. Members whose number WhatsApp hides are shown as Hidden number.

Does WAVault work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Business labels become filters and an export column, and business names are exported.

Does WAVault upload my data?

No. It has no server and makes no network requests of its own. Files are created in your browser and saved to your computer.

Can I back up WhatsApp chats with photos to my computer?

Yes. In Chats, pick the chats, formats and media types, and click Back up. You get a ZIP with a folder per chat, the media files and an offline viewer that looks like WhatsApp.

Is WAVault free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no account and no limits.
