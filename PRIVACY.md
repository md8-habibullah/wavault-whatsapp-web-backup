# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

WAVault is a browser extension that helps you back up and export your own WhatsApp Web data. This policy explains
what it does with that data. Short version: **it never leaves your computer.**

## What WAVault reads

When you open WAVault on `https://web.whatsapp.com`, it reads data that WhatsApp Web has already loaded in your
own browser tab: contacts, chats, groups and their members, messages, media and status updates. It reads them only
when you open the dashboard and only to show them to you and create the files you ask for.

## What WAVault stores

Only your preferences (chosen export format, columns, theme, whether the launcher tab is shown), in your browser's
extension storage (`storage` permission). It does not store contacts, messages or media. Files you export are saved
by your browser where you choose, and are yours.

## What WAVault sends

**Nothing.** WAVault has no server. It makes no network requests of its own, includes no analytics, tracking,
advertising or crash-reporting code, and loads no remote code. Media is downloaded and decrypted through WhatsApp
Web's own functions, the same way WhatsApp Web does when you open a photo.

## Sharing and selling

WAVault does not collect your data, so it does not share or sell it.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to `https://web.whatsapp.com/*` | To add the dashboard to WhatsApp Web and read your data there |
| `storage` | To remember your preferences |

## Children

WAVault is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
